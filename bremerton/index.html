Keeping Your Bremerton HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement in peak season.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement.
