Full-Service HVAC Care for Seattle Homes and Businesses

Heating and cooling around here means a wide mix of equipment: aging furnaces in established neighborhoods, high-efficiency heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and rooftop units on commercial buildings. Our team works on every one of those systems. Our technicians have years in the field, and we've learned how the weather here, the age of local homes, and local code affect the way equipment performs over time.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs are usually quick: a failed capacitor, a corroded flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a worn-out blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling its way to an early failure. Maintenance visits cover coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, which does more than anything else to extend equipment life.

Two situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Seattle

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.

Common HVAC Problems in Seattle

HVAC systems in this area face a familiar set of enemies. Long cooling seasons push compressors and capacitors to their limits, and salt air and moisture chew at outdoor condenser coils and fins, cutting efficiency years before the equipment should show its age. Dirty filters quietly starve systems of airflow, freezing evaporator coils in summer and overheating furnace heat exchangers in winter, a slow kind of damage most homeowners never see coming.

We also see the full range of component failures in local homes: weak capacitors that leave the condenser humming without starting, contactors with pitted points, cracked igniters and dirty flame sensors that lock furnaces out on cold mornings, slow refrigerant leaks that leave the house lukewarm by afternoon, and blower motors worn down by years of restricted airflow. The area's older housing stock adds its own layer, undersized returns, leaky or crushed ductwork, and additions that no duct run ever reached.

Every one of these problems is fixable, and most are much less expensive when caught early. If your system is doing anything unusual, strange noises, weak airflow, rooms that won't hold temperature, bills creeping upward, a quick diagnostic visit from our team catches the small problem before it becomes an expensive one.
