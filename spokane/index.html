Keeping Your Spokane HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Comfort Challenges Spokane Homeowners Face

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, a musty smell at startup. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
